# Reflect on 5stack behavior

Review the current session for repeated explanation preferences, confusion, interruptions, missing interruptions, useful or excessive ceremony, routing choices, praise, frustration, review burden, verification value, and appropriate or missing pushback.

Separate candidates into:

- confident 5stack feedback;
- ambiguous possible feedback;
- project-specific or non-5stack issues.

Do not save every observation. Record confident feedback in `.agent-journal/5stack-feedback.md` with source `retrospective` and the same fields used by `/feedback`. Deduplicate first.

Ask at most one concise question when an ambiguous candidate is important enough to resolve. Do not turn reflection into grilling.

Do not modify 5stack. End with a short list of recorded IDs and any unresolved candidate.
